## What was checked
27F, 7 weeks amenorrhoea, sudden collapse, BP82/54, HR128, peritonism, boggy cul-de-sac fullness — coherent haemorrhagic-shock-from-ruptured-ectopic picture.

- Discriminatory zone quoted as "1,500–3,500 mIU/mL, widened from the older 1,500–2,000 figure" — accurate and appropriately current; matches modern ACOG/transvaginal-ultrasound-era teaching that the older fixed 1,500–2,000 threshold has been revised upward and is operator/equipment-dependent.
- TVS findings (empty uterus with decidual reaction, adnexal mass, free fluid to Morison's pouch) and FAST scan are correctly used to justify going straight to theatre in an unstable patient without waiting for further imaging.
- CT abdomen is explicitly and correctly graded **harmful** for this specific (unstable, actively bleeding) patient — taking her out of the resuscitation bay for imaging that only confirms what is already known, correctly framed as a dangerous delay rather than a diagnostic benefit.
- Sequencing: `laparotomy` `requiresFirst: ['iv_access']`, with a harmful-sequence penalty for inducing anaesthesia before a line is running — correct anaesthetic safety teaching. Crystalloid/PRBC transfusion correctly framed as running in parallel with, never as a precondition for, surgery.
- PRBC transfusion correctly guided by clinical instability rather than a fixed haemoglobin threshold; O-negative blood used immediately if cross-match isn't ready — correct massive-transfusion/emergency-transfusion practice.
- Salpingectomy vs. salpingostomy discussion is accurate: salpingectomy preferred when the tube is extensively damaged/bleeding is not easily controlled (this patient); salpingostomy reserved for a stable patient with a healthy contralateral tube wanting fertility preservation, with serial beta-hCG follow-up — correct and appropriately nuanced.
- Anti-D immunoglobulin correctly indicated for this Rh-negative woman regardless of gestational age (sensitising event) — correct.
- Methotrexate correctly graded **harmful**: correctly reserved for a selected, stable patient with a small unruptured mass and low/plateauing beta-hCG; correctly identified as useless against active bleeding and dangerous as a time-waster in this unstable, ruptured patient.
